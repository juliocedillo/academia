One big academic family.

I was in office hours with Ricarda Hammer, who told me during a conversation that José Itzingsohn is my "academic grandpa" since I was mentored by both of his students. This gave me an idea to trace the scholars who advised and influenced my mentors, who lead to my academic interests.

This genealogy traces scholars beginning from the 1880s to 2026! From José Itzigsohn, the genealogy branches to Michael Rodríguez-Muñiz and Ricarda Hammer, who have both served as mentors in my intellectual development.

This is an evolving project and will continue to develop as I document my intellectual relationships and traditions.
