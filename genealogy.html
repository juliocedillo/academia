Tracing ideas through people.

Academic genealogy offers a way of tracing intellectual traditions through relationships between scholars, teachers, mentors, and students.

This genealogy traces a line from Franklin Henry Giddings through F. Stuart Chapin, William H. Sewell, Archibald O. Haller, Alejandro Portes, and José Itzigsohn.

From José Itzigsohn, the genealogy branches through my mentors Michael Rodríguez-Muñiz and Ricarda Hammer, whose mentorship has shaped my development as a sociologist.

The genealogy is an evolving project and will continue to develop as I document the intellectual relationships and traditions that inform my work.
