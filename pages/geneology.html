01 About the Genealogy

Tracing ideas through people.

Academic genealogy provides a way of understanding scholarship not only through institutions and publications, but through relationships between teachers and students.

This genealogy is intended to trace the intellectual lineages that inform my own work in sociology, particularly scholarship concerning race, the state, citizenship, political sociology, and Du Boisian sociology.
