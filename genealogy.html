01 About the Genealogy

Tracing ideas through people.

Academic genealogy provides a way of understanding scholarship not only through institutions and publications, but through relationships between teachers, mentors, and students.

This genealogy traces an intellectual lineage from Franklin Henry Giddings through F. Stuart Chapin, William H. Sewell, Archibald O. Haller, Alejandro Portes, and José Itzigsohn, before branching through my mentors Michael Rodríguez-Muñiz and Ricarda Hammer.

The genealogy is intended as an evolving representation of the intellectual relationships that inform my work in sociology.
